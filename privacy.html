Lexis Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexis ("Lexis", "the Bot", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, and handles information when you interact with Lexis through Discord.

2. Information Lexis Accesses

Lexis may access information made available through Discord's API when necessary to provide its features. Depending on the feature being used, this information may include:

  • Discord user and member identifiers
  • Discord server identifiers
  • Server roles and role membership
  • Channel and message identifiers when required by a command
  • Slash command and interaction information

Lexis does not use the Discord Presence Intent and does not monitor users' online status or activities.

3. Server Members Intent

Lexis uses Discord's Server Members Intent for role-management functionality.

For example, eligible members may select cosmetic color roles. When a member loses the required "Paid User" role, Lexis detects the member update and automatically removes associated paid-user color roles.

Member information accessed for this functionality is used only as necessary to provide and maintain the role-management system.

4. Message Content

Lexis does not use Discord's privileged Message Content Intent to monitor or collect general conversations within Discord servers.

Certain commands may operate on a specific Discord message when explicitly requested by an authorized user, such as replying to a message identified by a Discord message link or message ID.

5. Data Storage

Lexis does not maintain an off-platform database of Discord member profiles, member roles, presence information, or general message content.

Lexis may store operational configuration information required for the Application to function, such as product status information and identifiers associated with messages created by Lexis.

6. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Discord is used only as necessary to provide Lexis functionality, including:

  • Processing Discord slash commands and interactions
  • Managing eligible member roles
  • Maintaining server status information
  • Sending or replying to messages when explicitly requested
  • Operating and maintaining the Application

7. Sharing of Information

Lexis does not sell Discord user data. Lexis does not use Discord user data for advertising or user profiling.

Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, integrity, or operation of Lexis.

8. Data Retention

Discord member information used for role-management events is processed as needed to perform the requested functionality and is not maintained by Lexis as a persistent member database.

Operational configuration data may be retained for as long as necessary to operate Lexis.

9. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users may request information regarding data associated with their use of Lexis or request deletion of applicable stored information by contacting the Lexis administrators through the official Lexis Discord support server.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Lexis. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

Lexis is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements. Lexis does not knowingly collect personal information from users in violation of Discord's age requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to Lexis, its functionality, or applicable requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries, or data-related requests can be submitted through the official Lexis Discord support server.
